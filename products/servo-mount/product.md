# Problem

The stock servo bracket kept flexing under load, and the arm drifted a
few degrees every time the motor changed direction. Good enough for a toy,
not good enough for a repeatable machine.

# Dream

I wanted a mount that felt solid in the hand. No wobble. Something you
could bolt down once and forget about.

![](prototype.jpg)

# First Prototype

The first print was too thin around the screw holes and cracked on the
second install. Useful failure: it showed exactly where the stress went.

# Development

Three iterations later the walls were thicker, the holes were reinforced,
and the whole part printed without supports.

![](final.jpg)

# Sharing

The STL and the STEP source are attached so you can print it as-is or
change it to fit your own servo.

# Inspiration

Every machine I build starts with one part that has to be trustworthy.
This is that part.
